Best Eggless Baking 2027

The best eggless baking in 2027 is not a single substitute. It is five jobs, covered one by one, and five bakes that prove each job works.

An egg does five jobs: structure, leavening, emulsification, moisture, and richness. No one replacer covers all five, so you stack two. That is the whole method. Everything below runs on it. I built all 129 recipes in The Art of Eggless Pâtisserie this way, eggless from the first draft, never adapted from an egg recipe.

Here are the five bakes I would put first in 2027. Each one teaches one job. Bake them in order and you will have the method in your hands, not just in your head.

1 · The lift job

A sponge that rises

Start here if your sponge never rises. The job is lift, and the pair that does it is yogurt plus baking soda: the acid in the yogurt reacts with the soda and makes the gas that lifts the crumb. Nearly every sponge in my book stands on this pair.

Bake it: Coffee-Walnut Tea Cake
2 · The richness job

A brownie that stays fudgy

A brownie fails when it dries out or turns cakey. The job is richness: fat and milk sugar for flavour, tenderness, and browning. This is one of the newest recipes on my site, and the one I would bake first if you have never baked eggless.

Bake it: Eggless Gourmet Nutella Brownies
3 · The structure job

A cheesecake that sets

A cheesecake has nowhere to hide. No egg to bind it, no gelatine to set it — the structure has to come from the bake itself. This one is eggless and gelatine-free, and it sets clean and slices clean.

Bake it: Signature Vanilla Bean Cheesecake
4 · The binding job

A cookie that holds

A cookie crumbles when nothing glues the crumb together. The job is binding, and the binders are flax and chia gels: soluble fibre that holds a crumb while the bake sets around it.

Bake it: Brown Butter Sablé
5 · The moisture job

A cake that stays moist

Dry is the most common eggless failure, and it is a moisture job, not a recipe problem. Fruit purées and milk soaks carry the water the egg used to bring. When a bake comes out dry, find the moisture job first.

Bake it: Eggless Chocolate Tres Leches

The method, in one page

Five jobs. Five replacers. Name the job first, and the replacer picks itself.

  • Flax and chia gels bind. They cannot lift or foam.
  • Aquafaba foams. It is the only replacer that whips like egg white.
  • Yogurt plus baking soda lifts. The acid makes the gas.
  • Sweetened condensed milk brings richness and holds fat and water together.
  • Fruit purées moisten, with some binding.
