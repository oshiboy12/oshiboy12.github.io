Before we bake

The practical questions

What happens in a private class, who it is for, and what you need to begin.

How does a 1:1 class work?

Live on Zoom — we bake together, step by step. I watch, correct and guide you in real time until every stage is right.

Who are the classes for?

Working professionals and home bakers alike — from first-timers to pastry chefs sharpening their eggless technique.

What do I need for a class?

Your own kitchen, basic baking equipment, and the ingredient list I send you before the session.

Do you teach in person?

1:1 classes run on Zoom. Lectures and guest lectures are available both online and offline — message me to plan one.

How do I book?

Message me on Instagram with what you want to learn, and we will plan your session together.
