Refund & Cancellation Policy

If something goes wrong with your purchase, here is exactly what happens. No runaround.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The ebook

There are no refunds on the ebook. It is a digital file, and a downloaded file cannot be returned. If something goes wrong with your copy, we will make it right a different way: if the download link does not work, the file is corrupt or will not open, or you paid but never received the ebook, email thecurlychef.co@gmail.com with your payment receipt or payment ID and we will reshare a new file with you.

One-to-one classes

Classes cannot be rescheduled, and there are no refunds on classes. Please be sure of your time before you book. If we have to cancel a session, we will arrange a new time with you.

Consultations and lectures

Consultations and lectures are also non-refundable. Any other terms are confirmed in writing when you book.

The short version

There are no refunds on anything we sell. If your ebook file does not work, we reshare a new one. If we cancel a class, we arrange a new time.

Questions

Write to thecurlychef.co@gmail.com and we will sort it out with you directly.
