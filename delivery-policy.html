Delivery Policy

Nothing here ships in a box. The book is a file, the classes are on Zoom, and this page says how each one reaches you.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

No physical shipping

Everything we sell is digital or online. There is no printed book to post, no parcel, and no shipping charge.

The ebook

After you pay, the download link for The Art of Eggless Pâtisserie is sent to the email address you gave at checkout, usually immediately and always within 24 hours. If nothing arrives within 24 hours, check your spam or junk folder first, then write to thecurlychef.co@gmail.com and we will resend it. The file is large (all 129 recipes), so download it on Wi-Fi.

Classes

For one-to-one classes, the Zoom link and the ingredient list are sent to your email before your session.

Consultations and lectures

Details for consultations and lectures are confirmed with you by email or Instagram message when you book.

Delivery charges

None. There is nothing to ship, so there is nothing to charge for delivery.
