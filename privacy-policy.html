Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask for. Your email delivers your ebook — it is never sold.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address — to deliver the ebook, and to send Book 2 updates only if you sign up for them.
  • Your name — only if you give it to us, for example when you write to us or sign up.
  • Anonymous analytics — Google Analytics records which pages are visited, the type of device, and approximate location. It does not identify you personally.

We never collect card details. Payments go through Razorpay’s secure checkout, and your card details go to Razorpay only.

How we use it

Your email is used to deliver your ebook, to answer your messages, and — only if you signed up — to tell you when Book 2 news is ready. Analytics are used only to understand which pages readers find useful, so the site can be improved.

Who else sees it

Only the services needed to run this site: Razorpay (payments), Google Analytics (anonymous statistics), Web3Forms (the Book 2 notify form) and Cloudflare (hosting). We never sell your data, and we never share it for advertising.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits. You can block cookies in your browser settings and the site will still work.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to thecurlychef.co@gmail.com. If you signed up for Book 2 updates and change your mind, write to the same address and we will take you off the list.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Any question about your data: thecurlychef.co@gmail.com.
